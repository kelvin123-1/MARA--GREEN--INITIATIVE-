Our story · Launched in 2021

Mara Green Initiative is a community-driven environmental and social development movement founded by Lekini Sarisar, Ian Letuya and Winnie Maiguran. Since its launch in 2021, the initiative has championed community participation and a greener future.

Who we are

Rooted in nature. Growing through community.

We believe environmental care becomes powerful when people work together. Our focus is on growing healthy plants, encouraging tree planting and sharing practical knowledge that helps communities care for their surroundings.

  • Tree planting and landscape greening
  • Seedling nursery and plant care
  • Environmental awareness and education
  • Community and organisational partnerships
